AI & Artistic Creation

Core Judgment: In the AI era, everything is possible. There is no human ability that AI completely cannot replace.

  • Human aesthetic judgment can be learned by AI through practice guidance
  • Humans give prompts and feedback; AI autonomously completes creation and revision
  • Ultimately can fully meet human aesthetic requirements
  • In the future, "painters who can't paint" may emerge

Traditional Enterprise AI Transformation

🚩 Biggest Pain Point: Lack of Clear Vision

  • No clear target of what the enterprise will become after AI application
  • Can only advance through trial and error, investing resources but possibly gaining nothing
  • New AI startups have clear goals; they only need to solve how to achieve them
  • Venture capital environment separates risk; entrepreneurs don't bear failure risk

The current era is a golden age for AI entrepreneurship; what suffers are traditional enterprises that can't find direction

Fundamental Defects of 2nd Gen

Five "Uns"

  • Unsafe — Extremely vulnerable to tiny perturbations
  • Untrustworthy — Black-box, unexplainable
  • Uncontrollable — Behavior hard to predict
  • Unreliable — Poor generalization
  • Not Generalizable — Can only discriminate, not truly "recognize"

Computers "don't know where the horse is"
They only classify in feature vector space

AI & Artistic Creation

Core Judgment
In the AI era, everything is possible.
There is no human ability AI cannot replace.

  • Human aesthetic judgment
    can be learned by AI through practice
  • Humans give prompts and feedback;
    AI autonomously completes creation
  • Ultimately can fully meet human aesthetic requirements
  • Future "painters who can't paint" may emerge

This seemingly absurd trend will inevitably be realized
